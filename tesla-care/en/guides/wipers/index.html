Replacing wiper blades

Turn on Wiper Service Mode and swap the blades. Replace them at least once a year.

Driver side
26 in (650 mm)
Passenger side
19 in (475 mm)
Interval
At least yearly

By model year

Blades for both the earlier Model 3 (2017–2023) and the Highland (2024–) are sold in 26/19 in with a push-button fitting. Tesla doesn't publish the sizes, so measure your current blades before buying.

Steps

  1. Turn the wipers off and shift into Park. Service mode only turns on while the wipers are off.
  2. On the touchscreen, go to Controls > Service > Wiper Service Mode and turn it on. The wipers move up and stop. Menu names can vary a little by software version.
  3. Lay a towel on the windshield, then lift the wiper arm just enough to reach the blade. The arm doesn't lock up, so keep holding it.
  4. Press the locking tab and slide the old blade off the arm.
  5. Slide the new blade on until it clicks, then lower the arm gently onto the glass. The longer blade goes on the driver side.
  6. Do the other side the same way, then turn Wiper Service Mode off. The wipers return to their normal position.

Be careful

  • Don't lift the wiper arm further than needed. Damage from doing so may not be covered by the warranty.
  • Use blades that match the originals. The wrong blades can damage the wiper system and affect the rain sensor.
