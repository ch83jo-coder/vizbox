Adjusting tire pressure

Set the pressure on the label in the driver's door pillar, with the tires cold. Many Model 3s use 42 psi (about 290 kPa).

Target
Door pillar label (often 42 psi)
When
After 3+ hours parked
How often
Monthly and before long trips

Steps

  1. Open the driver's door and read the recommended pressure on the tire information label on the door pillar. The number on the tire sidewall is the maximum, not the target.
  2. Check with the tires cold: after 3+ hours parked, or before you drive. The car picture on the touchscreen also shows each tire's pressure.
  3. Unscrew the valve cap and press the gauge or inflator firmly onto the valve.
  4. Add air up to the target. If you overshoot, let a little out with the gauge's release button.
  5. Measure again to confirm, then screw the cap back on. Repeat for all four tires.
  6. Drive above 25 km/h (15 mph) for a short while; the screen updates to the new pressures and the warning light goes off.

Be careful

  • Don't let air out of warm tires to match the cold pressure. They'll be underinflated once they cool.
  • Don't reset the TPMS sensors to clear a pressure warning. The reset is for things like fitting different wheels.
