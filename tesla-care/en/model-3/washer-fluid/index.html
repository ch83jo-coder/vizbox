Topping up washer fluid

Just fill the reservoir behind the front trunk. Never use washer fluid that contains water repellent or bug wash.

Where
Behind the front trunk
Capacity
About 3.2 L
Time
About 5 min

On your Model 3 (earlier)

The earlier Model 3 manual lists a 3.2 L reservoir. The filler is at the back of the front trunk, toward the windshield.

Steps

  1. Park (P) on level ground and open the front trunk.
  2. Find the washer fluid filler cap at the back of the trunk, toward the windshield. The washer reservoir is the only one you top up; leave the other reservoirs under the maintenance panel alone.
  3. Wipe around the cap so no dirt falls in, then open it.
  4. Fill until you can see the fluid just below the filler neck. A funnel keeps it from spilling.
  5. Wipe up any spills, close the cap and the trunk, then test the spray with the wiper stalk.

Be careful

  • Don't use washer fluid with water repellent or bug wash. It causes streaking, smearing and squeaking.
  • Where temperatures drop below 4 °C (39 °F), use washer fluid with antifreeze.
