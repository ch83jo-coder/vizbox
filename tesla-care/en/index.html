Guides

  • Topping up washer fluidJust fill the reservoir behind the front trunk. Never use washer fluid that contains water repellent or bug wash.
  • Replacing wiper bladesTurn on Wiper Service Mode and swap the blades. Replace them at least once a year.
  • Adjusting tire pressureSet the pressure on the label in the driver's door pillar, with the tires cold. Many Model 3s use 42 psi (about 290 kPa).
