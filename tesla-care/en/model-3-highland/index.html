Guides

Topping up washer fluid

Just fill the reservoir behind the front trunk. Never use washer fluid that contains water repellent or bug wash.

About 5 min · Read the guide →

Replacing wiper blades

Turn on Wiper Service Mode and swap the blades. Replace them at least once a year.

About 10 min · Read the guide →

Adjusting tire pressure

Set the pressure on the label in the driver's door pillar, with the tires cold. Many Model 3s use 42 psi (about 290 kPa).
