Nagoya TOP10

Ōsu Kannon (大須観音)

A temple next to a lively shopping arcade. Plan your next trip to Nagoya with photos and maps.

Highlights

Formally named Kitanosan Shinpuku-ji Hōshō-in, this Shingon temple is counted among Japan's three great Kannon temples. It originally stood in what is now Hashima in Gifu Prefecture and was moved to its present site in Nagoya's castle town in 1612 on the orders of Tokugawa Ieyasu.

Its Shinpuku-ji library holds many rare texts, including the oldest surviving manuscript of the Kojiki. The surrounding Ōsu shopping streets are packed with street food, vintage clothing and electronics shops, so a visit pairs easily with a wander around town.

Getting there

Right next to Ōsu Kannon Station on the Tsurumai subway line.
