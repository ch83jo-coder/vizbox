Nagoya TOP10

Atsuta Shrine

A venerable shrine wrapped in deep greenery. Plan your next trip to Nagoya with photos and maps.

Highlights

Atsuta enshrines Kusanagi, the sacred sword that is one of Japan's three imperial regalia. Long revered as second only to Ise Grand Shrine, it draws more than two million people for the first shrine visit of the New Year.

The grounds sit in a forest of old trees, including a giant camphor, and feel remarkably quiet for the middle of a city. Look for the Nobunaga Wall, said to have been donated by Oda Nobunaga in thanks for his victory at Okehazama, and the Kusanagi-kan treasure hall that opened in 2021.

Getting there

Meitetsu's Jingu-mae Station is just east of the grounds. Atsuta Jingu Nishi and Atsuta Jingu Temmacho stations on the Meijo subway line and JR Atsuta Station are also within walking distance.
